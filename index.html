How To Get Your Stock File

Pull a fresh export from Pronto whenever you want current numbers — the figures here are only as recent as the last file loaded.

  1. Log into back of house Pronto.
  2. Select Inventory, then find Selective Inventory Enquiry.
  3. Enter your store number into warehouse.
  4. Press Enter through the other fields to accept them.
  5. Enter Y into "Only if QOH > 0".
  6. Set Max Search Count to 99999.
  7. Export the file, and make sure it saves as an Excel Workbook (.xlsx).
  8. Upload it with Choose stock file above.

Compare devices

Put two or three devices side by side — what they cost here today, and how they differ. Prices come from the same engine as the quote, so they are the prices you can actually give. Specifications are the Australian variants, which matters: an S26 sold here runs an Exynos 2600, not the Snapdragon the American reviews are written about.

What these terms mean
AMOLED / LTPO
The screen type. AMOLED lights each pixel on its own, so blacks are properly black. LTPO lets the screen slow itself down when nothing is moving, which saves battery.
120Hz
How many times a second the screen redraws. Higher looks smoother when scrolling. 60Hz is the old standard; 90Hz and 120Hz are noticeably smoother.
Nits
Brightness. The higher the number, the easier the screen is to read in direct sun.
Processor
The chip that runs everything. A faster one matters most for games, video and keeping the phone quick years from now.
Memory
Two different things in one line: RAM (how much the phone can juggle at once) and storage (how many photos, apps and videos fit). 12GB + 256GB means 12GB of RAM and 256GB of storage.
OIS
Optical image stabilisation — the lens physically moves to cancel out a shaky hand. It is the difference between a sharp photo and a blurry one indoors or at night.
Ultrawide / telephoto
Ultrawide fits more in the frame, for buildings and group shots. Telephoto zooms in without losing detail — “3×” means three times closer.
mAh
The size of the battery. Bigger usually means longer between charges, though a more efficient screen and chip matter just as much.
Watts (W)
Charging speed. 45W fills a phone far faster than 15W — but only with a charger that can supply it, which is not always in the box.
Qi2 and Qi2 Ready
Wireless charging standards. Qi2 has magnets in the phone, so chargers and accessories snap into place on their own. Qi2 Ready is Samsung’s term for a phone that does the same thing only once you put a magnetic case on it — the magnets are in the case, not the phone.
IP68, IP48, IPX8, IP54
Dust and water protection. The first number is dust (6 is fully sealed, 5 is mostly, 4 means bits over 1mm are kept out, X means untested); the second is water (8 means it survives being submerged, 4 only splashes). So IP68 is sealed against dust and survives a dunking, while IP48 survives the dunking but is not dust sealed.
Updates
How many new versions of Android the device is promised. Samsung releases roughly one a year, so seven updates is about seven years of staying current — and security patches usually continue for a similar stretch. Once they stop, the phone still works, but it stops getting new features and security fixes.

Wireless charging names the standard each device publishes. Qi2.2 carries the magnetic profile, so the magnets are in the phone; Samsung’s Qi2 Ready means Qi2 alignment only once a magnetic case is on it. GSMArena lists the standard but not the magnets, so that last part follows from what each standard requires rather than from the spec sheet. This is a quoting aid, not a source of truth. Offers and prices change, and some require codes or conditions applied at the register — always confirm the final price in POS at the time of the transaction. Always confirm the final price in POS at the time of the transaction.

How ECO vouchers work here. An ECO voucher is never taken off the device that earns it. Tick it on, and it is spent against an eligible Watch, Buds or Tablet elsewhere in the same quote — capped at what that product costs, with any unused balance called out. With no eligible ECO product in the quote, it stays listed but reduces nothing. Offers needing a unique code (Memory Upgrade, Samsung Wallet) only come off once ticked, since they're keyed in manually at POS.

Stacking rules. SMB replaces every other offer on a line (flat 15% off). Education applies the education rate and drops any sales-kit offer marked education/SMB-exclusive; for Watch9 and Watch Ultra 2 the boosted 25% rate replaces the standard 20% rather than adding to it. The wearable MBO (30% off a watch bundled with a Flip8/Fold8/Fold8 Ultra/S26) replaces all other offers on that watch. Fold/Flip ECO vouchers and trade-in bonuses cannot be combined — with a trade-in active the bonus is paid and the voucher is shown as unavailable. New Galaxy Club carries two separate offers: a $100 bonus off the device, and a plan discount that comes off the NGC price itself.

Printing. "Print quote" strips the page back to the customer quote — lines, credits and total, stamped Quote Created with today's date — then print it with your browser's own print command (Ctrl/⌘+P). "Copy quote" puts the same thing on your clipboard for pasting into an email.
